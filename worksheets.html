Lesson Ideas & Curriculum Tips for Teachers

Live Seismology Cross-Check: Have students open our Interactive 3D Map alongside this worksheet. Ask them to count how many earthquakes in the past 24 hours occurred along plate #1 (Pacific Plate) compared to plate #5 (Antarctic Plate). This illustrates why subduction zones generate the overwhelming majority of global tremors.

Plate Speed Calculations: Challenge students to calculate how many meters the Pacific Plate will travel over 10,000 years at its current speed of 8.1 cm per year (810 meters).
