About this Map

This blank tectonic plates map displays the precise boundary outlines of Earth's lithospheric plates without labels or text annotations. Designed specifically for geography classrooms, university earth science coursework, homeschool study, and student assessments, this outline map allows learners to test their knowledge of plate names, movement directions, and geological boundaries. Every boundary line corresponds strictly to the Peter Bird (2003) PB2002 digital model, ensuring scientific accuracy without geometric oversimplification. Students can label the seven major plates, locate smaller microplates, shade oceanic versus continental crust, or sketch active volcanic arcs and collision zones directly onto the map. The clean, high-contrast monochrome design ensures high legibility when printed on standard home or classroom printers. Download this educational outline map free in print-ready A4 or US Letter PDF formats, high-resolution 2400-pixel PNG for digital worksheets, or scalable vector SVG for custom teaching materials.
