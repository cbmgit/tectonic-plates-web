About this Map

Tectonic plate interactions occur along dynamic margins categorized into three primary structural types: convergent, divergent, and transform boundaries. This specialized plate boundary map visualizes the Earth's entire 5,819-step boundary network, color-coded according to fault kinematics based on the PB2002 dataset. Red boundary lines represent convergent margins, including deep subduction zones (such as the Mariana Trench, Sunda Arc, and Peru-Chile Trench) and massive continental collisions (such as the Indo-Eurasian Himalayan front). Yellow lines mark divergent boundaries, where new oceanic lithosphere generates along seafloor spreading ridges (like the Mid-Atlantic Ridge and East Pacific Rise) or continental rift zones (like the East African Rift). Green lines depict transform faults, where adjacent plates grind past each other horizontally along strike-slip fractures like California's San Andreas Fault and the Alpine Fault in New Zealand. An integrated visual legend provides clear guidance on boundary classifications.
